Find what each creature is weak to. Green cells take extra damage from that type, red cells resist it. Select a creature for its attacks and tiers. The link keeps your filters.

Weak to

Data extracted from the game files with tools/. Resistances are damage multipliers: ×2 means the creature takes double damage of that type, ×0.1 means it takes a tenth. Values are base stats before difficulty scaling. Biomes list where a creature spawns in survival maps; creatures with no biome appear only in story missions or events.
